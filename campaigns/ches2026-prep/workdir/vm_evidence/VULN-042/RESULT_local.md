## Finding
The AES module uses 5 RndCnst obfuscated constants in aes_pkg.sv:
1. `RndCnstClearingLfsrSeedDefault` (64-bit LFSR seed)
2. `RndCnstClearingLfsrPermDefault` (64-wide permutation)
3. `RndCnstClearingSharePermDefault` (64-wide permutation) **← NO documented generator**
4. `RndCnstMaskingLfsrPermDefault` (160-wide permutation)
5. `RndCnstMaskingLfsrSeedDefault` (288-bit Trivium seed)

Constants 1,2 are documented as generated by: `gen-lfsr-seed.py --width 64 --seed 31468618 --prefix "Clearing"`
Constant 4 is documented as generated by: `gen-lfsr-seed.py --width 160 --seed 31468618 --prefix "Masking"`
Constant 5 is documented as generated by: `gen-lfsr-seed.py --width 288 --seed 31468618 --prefix "Masking"`

**Constant 3 (RndCnstClearingSharePermDefault) has NO documented gen-lfsr-seed.py command.** Its value cannot be verified against the upstream generator. Its provenance is unknown.

The RTL integrity checks only verify permutation bijectivity (PermutationCheck_A assertion in aes_prng_clearing.sv and aes_prng_masking.sv). A tampered-but-bijective permutation would pass this check. LFSR seeds have no check at all.
